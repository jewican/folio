OCTOBER 13, 2026 — CHANGE IN THE PROSECUTION'S CASE

The House impeachment prosecution announced on October 13, 2026 that it would no longer pursue three of the four impeachment articles against Vice President Sara Z. Duterte. House lead prosecutor Representative Gerville "Jinky Bitrics" Luistro informed the Senate impeachment court of the decision.

The prosecution said it would cease pursuing these articles:

- Article I — allegations concerning the misuse of confidential funds.
- Article III — allegations concerning bribery.
- Article IV — allegations concerning grave threats.

The prosecution said it would continue on one article:

- Article II — allegations concerning unexplained wealth.

PROSECUTION'S REASONING

The House prosecution stated that, in its assessment, evidence relating to Article II was sufficient to support proceeding with that article on its own. Its position was that the remaining charge could justify the Vice President's removal from office. This is the prosecution's assessment, not an independent finding of guilt.

CHANGE RELATIVE TO THE EARLIER DOCUMENTS

Before this announcement, the impeachment case presented four articles. The announcement narrowed the prosecution's intended case to Article II. This is a procedural change in the charges the prosecution would pursue; it does not by itself prove or disprove the allegations in the withdrawn articles, nor establish guilt on the remaining article.
